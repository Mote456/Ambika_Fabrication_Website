Poultry Farm Sheds

We design and construct high-capacity steel poultry farm sheds engineered to maintain an optimal controlled climate. Our structures promote superior ventilation, biosecurity, and natural lighting to maximize flock health and egg/broiler production efficiency.

Every shed is custom-fabricated with anti-corrosive galvalume steel and heavy-duty trusses designed to withstand harsh rural weather, moisture, and high winds.

Onion Storage Sheds

Specialized agricultural storage structures engineered specifically to preserve onion harvests and perishable produce. Featuring multi-tier ventilated steel racking systems and high air velocity designs that minimize rot, weight loss, and spoilage.

Our heavy-duty steel frameworks allow for multi-ton storage capacity while ensuring rain protection and continuous cross-ventilation across all storage tiers.

Multi-Tier Heavy-Duty Ventilated Storage Racks
Forced Air & Natural Wind Airflow Channels
Weather-Shielded Slanted Steel Roofing
Perforated Side Slats for Moisture Removal
High Tonnage Load-Bearing Structural Framing
Custom Capacity Designs (10 MT to 500+ MT)

Industrial Sheds

We engineer and erect pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB) and industrial factory sheds compliant with IS 800 standards. Our structures offer maximum clear span, optimized column spacing, and robust durability for manufacturing plants and logistics hubs.

All industrial structures utilize IS 2062 certified high-strength steel, complete with structural load calculations and weather-resistant cladding systems.

Warehouses & Distribution Facilities
