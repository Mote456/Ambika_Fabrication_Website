Service 01

Tensile Structures

Our tensile membrane structures combine engineering rigor with visual elegance. Using high-tensile PVC/PTFE membranes and precision steel sub-structures, we create durable, low-maintenance canopies that withstand wind loads, UV exposure, and heavy rainfall.

From intimate gazebo covers to large-scale stadium roofing — every project is custom-engineered to site conditions and client aesthetic preferences.

Car Parking Canopies — Single & Multi-bay configurations
Gazebos & Leisure Shades for resorts and residences
Stadium & Sports Complex Covers
Walkway Canopies & Pedestrian Bridges
Swimming Pool & Poolside Shades
Entry Portal & Facade Canopies
✅ High-Tensile Membranes

Service 02

Industrial Fabrication

We design and erect pre-engineered steel buildings and factory sheds that meet the highest structural standards. Our industrial structures are engineered for maximum usable span, optimized column spacing, and long-term durability in demanding industrial environments.

All our industrial structures use IS 2062 certified steel, comply with IS 800 design code, and are backed by full structural drawings and load calculations.

Factory Sheds & Production Halls
Warehouses & Logistics Centers
Steel Roofing & Cladding Systems
Mezzanine Floors & Platforms
Cold Storage Structures
Multi-Bay Industrial Complexes
✅ IS 2062 Steel
✅ IS 800 Compliant
