About Company

Engineering Strength & Crafting Excellence Since 2000

Ambika Fabrication Works is one of Maharashtra's premier structural steel fabrication companies, specializing in high-grade agricultural sheds, tensile membrane structures, industrial factory buildings, and onion storage units.

With two decades of industry experience, our state-of-the-art 50,000 sq.ft. manufacturing plant is equipped with precision CNC cutting, heavy-duty welding rigs, and dedicated structural engineers delivering pan-India turnkey solutions.
