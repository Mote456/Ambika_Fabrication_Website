Our Story

Forging Excellence Since 2005

Founded in 2005 by a team of passionate Fabrication in Pune, Ambika Fabrication began with a single workshop and an unwavering commitment to quality. What started as a small operation serving local industrial clients has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading fabrication companies.

Today, we operate a 50,000 sq.ft. state-of-the-art manufacturing facility equipped with advanced CNC machinery, robotic welding systems, and a dedicated R&D team. Our projects span across India — from car parking canopies in Mumbai to large-scale stadium tensile structures in Bengaluru.

ISO 9001:2015 certified fabrication processes with strict quality control at every stage.
Team of 150+ skilled engineers, certified welders, and project managers.
Pan-India project delivery with on-site erection and commissioning services.
850+ successfully completed projects with zero structural failure record.

Quality & Safety

Certified Standards. Zero Compromise.

Every structure we build adheres to stringent quality, safety, and environmental standards — because lives depend on what we build.

🏅 ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Our entire fabrication workflow is ISO certified — from raw material procurement and welding procedures to final inspection and dispatch.

⚙️ Certified Welding Standards

All welders are certified under AWS D1.1 and IS 9595 standards. We use UT, RT, and PT non-destructive testing on all critical welds.

📐 CAD Design Precision

Every project begins with detailed AutoCAD and STAAD.Pro structural analysis. We fabricate only after engineering approval of load calculations and drawings.

🦺 Rigorous Safety Protocols

Zero-compromise site safety with mandatory PPE, daily toolbox talks, fall protection systems, and compliance with IS 7969 safety standards.

🔬 Material Verification

All steel is IS 2062 grade certified. We conduct mill test certificate verification and third-party material testing on every major project.

♻️ Sustainable Practices

We minimize material waste through optimized nesting, recycle steel offcuts, and use eco-friendly surface treatments wherever possible.
