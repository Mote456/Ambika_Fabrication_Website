Our Story

Forging Excellence Since 2000

Founded in 2000 by a Somnath Shirsath in Nimon, Sangamner. Ambika Fabrication began with a single workshop and an unwavering commitment to quality. What started as a small operation serving local industrial clients and local farmer clients has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading fabrication companies.

Today, we operate a 50,000 sq.ft. state-of-the-art manufacturing facility equipped with advanced CNC machinery, robotic welding systems, and a dedicated R&D team. Our projects span across India — from car parking canopies in Mumbai to large-scale stadium tensile structures in Bengaluru.

ISO 9001:2015 certified fabrication processes with strict quality control at every stage.
Team of skilled engineers, certified welders, and project managers.
Pan-India project delivery with on-site erection and commissioning services.
850+ successfully completed projects with zero structural failure record.
